# Feature: Part Templates and Variants

**Docs:** [Templates](https://docs.inventree.org/en/stable/part/template/) · [Revisions — template restrictions](https://docs.inventree.org/en/stable/part/revision/#revision-restrictions) · [BOM inheritance](https://docs.inventree.org/en/stable/manufacturing/bom/#inherited-bom-line-items)

A Template part can have variants. Variant serials must be unique across the family. Template stock includes variant stock. Templates cannot have revisions; variants can.
